FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need every portal?

No. Use the one that matches your work. They share one record, so they connect when you add more.

Where does Atlas fit?

Atlas is the base. Its 3D roof model appears inside Studio and Connect.

Do I still need a site visit?

Not to start. Sites that need precision still get a field check, and the data goes back into the same record.

What does it cost?

Free during beta.

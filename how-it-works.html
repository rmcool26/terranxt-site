How it works

From satellite view to working plant.

pvNXT follows a solar project from the first look at a roof to the last alert on the plant. Each step uses the same project data.

Seven steps.

01Atlas

Satellite imagery becomes a 3D roof model.

02Field check

A survey team confirms details on site, when needed.

03Connect

The customer checks feasibility and compares quotes.

04Studio

The EPC team designs the layout and prepares the BOQ.

05Proposal

The customer proposal is generated from the design.

06Go

Installers carry out the work and report from the roof.

07SCADA

The plant is monitored, and every alert has an owner.

Every tool reads and writes the same project record. Nothing is retyped, and every change is logged.

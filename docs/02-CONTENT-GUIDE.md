# Content guide

## Voice
English, short sentences, active voice, plain words. One line, one idea. Write as an experienced writer explaining to an engineer. No hype words (revolutionary, seamless, game-changing), no emoji, no "AI-powered" labels.

## Claims policy
- No numbers we have not measured. Measured numbers can be added later (time to design, projects run, etc.).
- Allowed facts: Atlas plus five portals, Android and iOS apps, IIT Delhi–FITT since 2023, Astongreens 10+ years in the field, in use on live Astongreens projects, free during beta.
- Artha: "Early access", "Register interest", risk statement, no returns or guarantee wording. Legal approves the final text.
- Atlas turnaround "typically within 24 hours". Confirm it is a real SLA.

## Who each portal is for (use these words everywhere)
Studio: I run a solar business (EPC companies, solar sellers). Go: I install solar. Connect: I want to go solar (homes, businesses, any property). SCADA: I run or monitor a plant. Artha: I want to invest.
Button labels: Open Studio, Open Go, Open Connect, Open SCADA, Join early access. Secondary: Learn about X.

## Headlines (live)
- Hero: Plan it by morning. Watch it run by evening.
- Problem: Solar projects get lost between tools.
- How it works: From satellite view to working plant. (Scan, Design, Deliver)
- Find your portal: Which one is yours?
- Proof: Built where solar actually gets installed.
- Closing CTA: Start with one project.

## Page CTAs
Home: Watch the 2-minute demo (hero), Choose your portal (closer). Product pages: Open the portal. Atlas: Request a 3D model. Artha: Join early access. Demo: Confirm booking. Careers: Submit. Investors: Request the deck.
